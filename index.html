Why performance-based, and how it actually works

You pay a base fee plus a percentage of incremental revenue.

Base fee

Covers our team: strategy lead, media buyer, creative producer and analyst. Senior operators every week, not junior account managers.

Incremental, not total

Our share is calculated only on revenue above your cohort baseline, so we earn on growth we create, not on what you'd make anyway.

Cap and floor

The variable share is capped, so there are no surprise bills, and floored, so the work keeps fair value if numbers stall for reasons outside our control. Both sides are protected.
