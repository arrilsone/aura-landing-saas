Why performance-based, and how it actually works.

Base fee % of incremental revenue

Base fee

Covers the team: strategy lead, media buyer, creative producer, analyst. Senior operators every week, not junior account managers.

Incremental, not total

Our share is calculated only on revenue above a cohort baseline set before we start. Growth you would have had anyway stays yours.

Cap and floor

The variable share is capped, so there are no surprise bills, and floored, so you pay fair value even if numbers stall outside our control. Both sides protected.

Frequently asked questions

Who do you work with?

Seed and Series A startups, mostly SaaS and consumer apps. The team has scaled companies in logistics, delivery, dating, fintech, payments and health-tech.

What happens on the free growth audit call?

60 minutes. We look at your funnel, ask the hard questions, and leave you with three things to change. Zero commitment.

What if the Diagnostic says No-Go?

Then you hear it before you spend on media. If the LTV/CAC math doesn't work, the written recommendation says so and explains what would have to change first.

How does the performance fee work?

A base fee covers the team. On top of that we take a share of incremental revenue, measured against a cohort baseline. The share is capped and floored, so neither side is exposed.

How many channels will you run?

Two to three, not seven. We run real tests with 5 to 8 creative angles per cycle, kill what fails fast and scale what proves traction.

Do you replace our in-house team?

No. We operate the engine while it is being proven, and the Scale Strategy stage includes a hiring plan: which roles to bring in-house, and when.

I'm an investor. How can we work together?

If you back venture-stage startups, set up a partner call. We'll outline portfolio benefits and share reference clients.
